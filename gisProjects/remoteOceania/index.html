Maps of Pacific islands

This interactive webmap was created during my Master's programme as part of a module in collaboration with Michael Zurmühle, accompanied and supervised by Peter Ranacher and Hedvig Skirgard.

The first map gives a general overview of the situation of the islands in the Pacific region during climate change and the associated rise in sea level.

The second map visualises the language groups present in the area and visualises the threat to linguistic diversity.
